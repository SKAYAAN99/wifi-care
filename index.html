Honest Service Scope

What we do vs. what we don't

Remote help is available across India. In-person visits are only offered locally, when available.

✓
What We Do
Router help, done by hand
  • Router setup: Help setting up a router, Wi-Fi names and passwords, and 2.4GHz / 5GHz options.
  • Slow Wi-Fi: Simple checks, router placement tips, and advice for Wi-Fi weak spots.
  • Connection drops: We help check whether the issue is your router settings or your ISP.
  • Price first: We explain the work and agree on price before charging you.
✕
What We Don't Do
And we'll tell you upfront
  • Damaged outside fibre cables: We cannot repair street fibre cables. Your ISP needs to send its cable team.
  • No fake speed promises: We can't guarantee speeds beyond your internet plan.
  • Private passwords: We don't need banking OTPs or payment passwords. Never send those.
  • No surprise charges: We tell you the price first. If the job changes, we check with you before continuing.
